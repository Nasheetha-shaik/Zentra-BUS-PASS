Faculty & Staff Privilege

University professors, lecturers, researchers, and campus administrative staff are entitled to a 100% subsidized transit benefit.

  • Zero transport fee required for employee bus pass.
  • Instant authorization with valid Employee ID.
  • Same boarding points and routes as student passes.
